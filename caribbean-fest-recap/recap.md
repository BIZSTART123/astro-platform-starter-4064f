## Full recap (newsletter / website / email)

**Caribbean Fest brought the island spirit home**

This past Saturday, Caribbean Fest turned our sanctuary into a celebration of Caribbean culture, faith and community.

The room looked the part from the moment you walked in. Emerald-green drapes were swagged with orange and gold, a cross hung over the stage, golden mums lined the front, and Jamaican flags hung beside the band. Guests came dressed for it too, in madras plaid, red head wraps, African-print skirts and plenty of black, green and gold.

The program moved between spoken performances and music, backed by a live band with drums and keys:

- **Spoken word and dramatic pieces.** Performers in madras plaid trousers and dark jackets held the stage. One acted out his piece, walking the stage, stepping down into the room and working the crowd.
- **Solo ministry.** A vocalist in a red-and-purple scarf ministered from centre stage.
- **The vocal ensemble.** This was the high point of the night. A group in green Jamaica jackets, white blouses, bright print skirts and plaid had the whole house on its feet.

By the end, people were dancing in the aisles and waving flags, and the selfies were rolling. It was church, a cultural celebration and a family reunion all at once.

A big thank you to our performers, musicians, decorating team, volunteers and everyone who came out to celebrate with us. Caribbean Fest was a reminder of how rich our heritage is and how good it is to celebrate it together.

*See you next year!*
