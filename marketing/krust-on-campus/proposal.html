The plan in brief

Most students at Mount Saint Mary College and SUNY Orange's Newburgh campus can't easily get to Route 17K between classes. Krust on Campus turns that into the offer: students pool an order with a study group, club, or floor, and Golden Krust brings it to one approved campus pickup point, Monday through Friday. Pooled orders reach the delivery minimum without anyone overspending, and each delivery run carries a full order.

  • Students: five campus deals named for class breaks, study sessions, club meetings, and events.
  • Plaza employees: a badge-holder offer timed to your slow hours, plus break-room catering from your existing team-lunch menu.
  • Faculty and departments: served through your existing catering menu and terms, kept separate from student pricing.
  • Food trucks and caterers: a wholesale pilot for patties, coco bread, and bun-and-cheese packs, once franchise and licensing questions are answered.

Before anything goes public, Unique needs to settle three things: real unit costs (the calculator below shows the contribution once they're entered), each college's outside-food rules, and franchisor approval for wholesale.

What the research changed

I checked the public menus for the Newburgh store and the college websites. Three pricing problems showed up that the original deal sheet doesn't account for.

1. The Between-Class Bite may cost more than buying the same items today

Delivery apps list this store's mild beef patty at $4.29–$4.50, coco bread at $1.50, and a patty-with-coco-bread combo at $5.59. At $6.99, the "deal" is $1.00–$1.40 above those prices, and app prices usually run higher than in-store ones. The Study Partner Combo ($13.49) has the same problem. Check your in-store POS price first. If $6.99 is above it, either include a drink at $6.99 or price the Bite at the regular combo price and let delivery be what makes it a deal.

2. The Study Group Pack undercuts your own patty catering box

goldenkrustnewburgh.com sells 12 patties for $72 (the "Department" tier). At $49.99 for 12, the student pack is about 31% cheaper. Faculty and offices will order the student pack instead. Keep it student-only: verify student or club status and keep it on the campus delivery program, so the $72 box stays the price for departments and businesses.

3. Club trays are priced well below your team-lunch packages

At $129 for 8–10 servings, the Club Meeting Tray works out to $12.90–$16.13 per serving. Your published team meals start at $25 per person. That gap suits student clubs, but the trays should stay restricted to student groups. Department lunches go through your existing catering menu.

4. Campus permission comes first

Mount Saint Mary College runs dining through Parkhurst Dining, and SUNY Orange has its own food service. I found no public outside-food or catering policy for either school. Ask Student Engagement (MSMC) and the Center for Student Involvement (SUNY Orange) which pickup points are allowed and whether clubs may order outside food. Don't print college names or set pickup points until they answer.

5. Public hours don't match

Listings show 7 AM–10 PM (corporate locator), 9 AM–10 PM Mon–Sat (goldenkrustnewburgh.com), about 8:15 AM–7:45 PM (Apple Maps), and 8:30 AM–7:10 PM for online ordering. Students who find the wrong hours become a lost order. Update Google, Apple Maps, the locator, and the delivery apps to one set of hours before launch.

Delivery runs Monday through Friday to approved Newburgh campus pickup points. It is not limited to one campus day.

  • Pooling is allowed. The minimum applies to the combined order, not to each student.
  • One order, one organizer, one spot. One person places and pays for the order. It goes to one approved pickup point in one delivery window.
  • Thresholds apply after discounts and before tax.
  • Coverage: within five road miles of the store, pending a timed test drive to each campus. Both campuses appear to be inside that radius, but the routes haven't been driven or measured. Unverified
  • Trays and Feasts need 24 hours' notice. Patty deals need a same-day cutoff (suggested: 10:00 AM for the lunch window, 2:00 PM for the late-afternoon window).
  • Suggested windows: 11:45 AM–12:15 PM and 4:30–5:00 PM. Set them from your staffing, cap each window at a fixed number of orders, and expand only when the calculator shows the runs pay.
  • Pickup points are only places each college approves in writing. The organizer meets the driver there. Food isn't left unattended.
  • No delivery when a college closes, on its breaks, or in unsafe weather. Orders move to the next window or get a full refund.

Keep the existing lunch program separate. Your current team-lunch and catering packages (published on goldenkrustnewburgh.com) keep their own prices, minimums, and terms. Krust on Campus rules apply only to the five campus deals above and only to student orders delivered to campus pickup points.

Ordering, payment, pickup, cancellation Proposal

  1. Gather the group. The organizer collects what everyone wants and checks that the combined subtotal clears $75 for delivery.
  2. Place one order. Call 845-787-4527 (or use an order form, if Unique sets one up) by the cutoff. Give the deal names, quantities, campus, pickup point, window, and a mobile number. Mention code CAMPUS so the order gets the campus price and can be tracked.
  3. Pay up front. The organizer pays the full amount by card when ordering. Clubs can pay with a club or college card if their school allows it. Students settle shares among themselves. Golden Krust takes one payment per order.
  4. Get a confirmation. Golden Krust confirms the total, window, and pickup point by text or email. Trays are confirmed at least 24 hours ahead.
  5. Meet the driver. The driver texts about 10 minutes out and waits up to 10 minutes at the pickup point. The organizer checks the order on handoff.

Cancellations and changes

  • Patty deals: free changes or cancellation until the order cutoff. After that, no refund once prep starts.
  • Trays and Feasts: full refund if cancelled 24+ hours before the window. 50% refund inside 24 hours. No refund once out for delivery.
  • No-show at pickup after 10 minutes: the order goes back to the store for pickup until close. No refund.

If something goes wrong

  • Missing or wrong items: report at handoff. We replace them on the next window or refund them.
  • Late by 20+ minutes because of us: the delivery fee is refunded.
  • College closure or weather: move the order or get a full refund.

Other sales programs

Plaza employees Proposal

Start with Target and two or three neighboring businesses whose managers agree.

  • Badge-holder offer during your slowest weekday hours, chosen from POS data. Example: free coco bread with any patty, 2–5 PM, Monday–Thursday. Avoid a standing percentage discount at peak lunch.
  • Break-room catering for shift meetings and staff appreciation, from your existing team-lunch menu and terms.
  • Ask permission before posting a flyer in any break room. Large retailers often have rules on outside solicitation.

Faculty and departments

  • Serve them from the existing catering menu (team meals from $25 per person, patty boxes 6 for $36 and 12 for $72, per goldenkrustnewburgh.com), not student pricing.
  • Department admins usually pay by purchase order or a college card, so expect vendor setup paperwork (W-9, possibly a certificate of insurance).
  • Offer a first-order tasting box for department assistants: the people who actually place lunch orders.

Clubs and campus events

  • Club Meeting Tray, Campus Event Feast, and Study Group Dozen.
  • Calendar hooks: finals study sessions (December), spring involvement fairs (MSMC holds one at the start of each semester), Black History Month programs (February), cultural nights, and end-of-year banquets.
  • Club funding at SUNY Orange runs through the Student Senate, so treasurers need an itemized quote ahead of time.

Food trucks and caterers: wholesale pilot Proposal

  • Products: mild-beef patty cases, coco bread packs, bun-and-cheese packs. Pricing comes from real costs, with a minimum order and a set weekly pickup day.
  • Get franchisor approval in writing first. Golden Krust franchise terms may limit reselling branded products to other businesses. Unverified
  • Confirm licensing. Selling food to other businesses in New York may need a NYS Agriculture & Markets license, plus labeling with ingredients and allergens. Ask Ag & Markets and the Orange County Health Department. Unverified
  • Check whether prospects can already buy frozen patties through distributors. That sets the price ceiling.

30 / 60 / 90-day rollout

The timing follows the academic calendar: finals arrive in December, winter break empties both campuses, and spring semester is the real launch. Confirm both colleges' calendars before setting dates.
